Privacy policy - Jarvis

Last updated: 27 October 2026

Jarvis is a private personal assistant used only by its owner, Muhammad Bahauddin, on his own computer. It is not offered to anyone else. This page explains what Jarvis does with Google user data.

What Google data Jarvis uses

  • Gmail (read-only): reads the owner's own messages to list new mail, sort it (important / needs a reply / other) and find job alert emails.
  • Gmail (compose): only creates reply drafts the owner asked for and approved. Jarvis never sends, deletes, archives or labels email.
  • Google Calendar (events): lists the owner's events; adds, moves or deletes an event only after the owner approves that exact change.
  • YouTube: uploads the owner's own videos to his own channel and reads their comments and statistics, only after the owner approves each post.

Where it is stored

Data is kept on the owner's own computer (a local database and files). Sign-in tokens stay on that computer. There is no server, website account or cloud database run by Jarvis.

Who it is shared with

Google user data is not sold, rented or shared with anyone, and is not used for advertising. To sort or summarise the owner's own email, short parts of a message may be sent to an AI model service for processing on the owner's behalf; it is used only to give the owner that result. Security and verification-code emails are never sent to AI.

Limited use

Jarvis's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

The owner can remove Jarvis's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions; local data is deleted by removing it from his computer.
