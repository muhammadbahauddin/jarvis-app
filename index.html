Jarvis

A private, single-user personal assistant run by Muhammad Bahauddin on his own computer.

Jarvis is not a public product and has no other users. It helps its owner with his own day: reading his own Gmail inboxes (read-only) and saving reply drafts he asks for, showing and changing events in his own Google Calendar after his approval, and uploading his own videos to his own YouTube channel after his approval.

Everything runs on the owner's PC. Nothing is sent, posted or changed without the owner's click.
